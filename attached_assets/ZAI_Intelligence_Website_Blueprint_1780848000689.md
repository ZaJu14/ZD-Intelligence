## 10. DESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS

### Aesthetic Direction

**Executive Refined Minimalism** — the design language of premium professional services firms (McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte Digital), adapted for a modern AI context. Clean, authoritative, confident. Not sterile. Not flashy.

### Key Design Principles

- Generous whitespace signals confidence and clarity
- Typography carries most of the visual weight
- Color is used sparingly and purposefully
- Every element earns its place on the page
- Mobile-first, but optimized for executive desktop viewing

### Layout System

- Max content width: 1200px
- Grid: 12-column, 24px gutter
- Section padding: 80–100px vertical
- Component spacing: 8px base unit (8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64px)

| **Accent Light**   | Gold Light | `#D4AB5C` | Hover states, soft accents         |

| **Surface**        | Mist       | `#F7F8FA` | Alternating section backgrounds    |

| **Text Secondary** | Slate      | `#4A5568` | Secondary text, descriptions       |

### Color Usage Rules

- Navy + Gold: hero, CTAs, trust-heavy sections
- White + Navy text: primary content sections
- Mist + Navy text: alternating content sections
- Gold accent: borders, numbers, icons — never used for large fills in light sections
- Never use pure black (#000000) — always use Navy or Charcoal

## 14. FAQ SECTION

### Home Page FAQs

1. **How do I know if my business is ready for AI?**  
   Most businesses are ready to benefit from AI — but not all AI projects are the right fit at the right time. Our discovery call is designed specifically to answer this question for your organization. We look at team readiness, process maturity, data quality, and risk tolerance to give you an honest answer.

2. **What makes ZAID Intelligence different from other AI consultants?**  
   Most AI consultants focus purely on capability — what AI can do. We focus equally on what AI should do in your context, and what guardrails are needed. Our founder's background in cybersecurity means governance and risk management are never an afterthought.

3. **Do you work with companies that have no AI experience?**  
   Absolutely. In fact, many of our most successful engagements start from zero. Beginning without preconceptions often makes adoption smoother. We build your team's literacy and confidence from the ground up.

4. **Can you run workshops for large teams?**  
   Yes. Our workshops are designed to scale. We run sessions for executive leadership groups of 5–10, department teams of 20–50, and all-hands sessions for 100+. Content and format are always tailored to audience seniority and role.

5. **What does a discovery call involve?**  
   It's a focused 45-minute conversation — no sales pitch, no obligation. We ask questions about your business, your AI goals, your current challenges, and your team. At the end, you'll have a clearer picture of what AI adoption could look like for you, and whether we're the right partner to help.

6. **Do you offer ongoing retainer support?**  
   Yes. For clients who want continuous guidance as the AI landscape evolves, we offer monthly advisory retainers. These include policy updates, tool evaluations, team Q&A sessions, and strategic check-ins.
